# Go Pray: the pledge

Last updated: 2026-11-12

**Half of what Go Pray earns after Apple's cut goes to charity until our costs for the year are covered, then all of it. Resets every 1 January. Receipts are public.**

**Paying is optional. The lock is free for everyone.** Prayer times, the Athan, Qibla and the lock all work either way.

Max and I make Go Pray. This page is our promise about the money, in plain words.

## How it works

- Apple takes its cut of every sale first. We count it as 30%.
- Half of what's left goes to charity.
- Once our costs for the year are covered, all of it goes to charity.
- Every 1 January it starts again at half.

**"Our costs"** means what it costs our family to build Go Pray and to live for the year. We publish only the percent covered, never the amount.

On the yearly plan ($19.99), that's about $7 of your plan going to charity.

## Your share goes to your cause

In the app you pick one of four causes. If you pay, your share goes to that cause. You can change it any time in Settings, Charity.

| Cause | What it pays for | Published cost of one |
|---|---|---|
| Children in crisis | Food and care for children in places like Gaza, Sudan and DR Congo | About $100 to treat 1 child for severe hunger |
| Girls' hygiene | Period kits for girls and women | $15 for 1 girl's kit |
| Kids' education | Books and learning for children | $50 for 1 child's reading year |
| Clean water | Wells and safe water for families | About $20 for 1 person's clean water |

These costs come from charities' own published prices, so every number here is an estimate. For example: 1,000 families on the yearly plan would cover 466 girls' kits.

Your pick is saved with RevenueCat, the service that tells the app whether a plan is active, next to an anonymous id with no name or email. You don't have to sign in to anything. If you haven't picked, your share is split equally across all four causes. If you reinstall the app, pick your cause again.

## Who gets the money

We aren't partnered with any charity yet. We split every payment in two as it comes in and keep a running total of the charity share. That money waits until we sign with a charity. When we do, we'll name it here.

## Tax

Your subscription is a purchase of the Go Pray Plan. It isn't a payment to a charity, so it isn't tax-deductible.
