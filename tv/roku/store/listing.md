## Long description
Go Pray plays the Athan on your TV at every prayer time.

When the time comes, the screen shows a quiet painted scene for that prayer, in English and Arabic, and the Athan plays through your TV speakers. When it ends, the channel closes by itself and takes you back to the Home screen. There is nothing to set up on the TV and nothing to press.

Go Pray works with the free Go Pray app on your Mac or Windows PC. Download the app, then add this TV in Go Pray > Settings > TVs. The app decides when it is prayer time and tells the TV.

- Five prayers: Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha
- Many voices to choose from, Sunni and Shia, picked in the Go Pray app
- Fajr uses the Fajr Athan of Sheikh Hamed
- No account, no ads, no timers, no "I prayed" buttons on the TV

Opening the channel by hand shows a short welcome screen with a link to the Go Pray app. Press Back to leave.

Go Pray collects no data.
